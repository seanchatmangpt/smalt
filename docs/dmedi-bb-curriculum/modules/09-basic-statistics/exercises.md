# Module 09 Exercises

Answers in `solution-key.md`; numbers court-checked in `courts/court.py`.

1. Mean 12.00946 vs median 12.00955: justify the mean as the center statistic for the
   capability math, and state when the median would replace it.
2. Compute the Tukey fences and list the flagged bores. How many, and are any outside
   spec (11.975–12.025)?
3. A teammate deletes the four fence-flagged bores "to clean up the tails." Compute
   what this does to s and to the capability estimate direction, without re-running
   Minitab — argue the direction only.
4. Centered process with s = 0.0074: what is P(below LSL)? Give the exact Minitab
   cumulative-probability answer.
5. Same spread, measured mean 12.0098: what are P(below LSL) and P(above USL)? Which
   limit binds, and why does centering help?
6. Predict sd of the sample mean for n = 5 from sigma = 7.407 um. Then state the two
   simulated values (seed 42009 process sim; seed 42010 CSV resample) and their
   percent deviation from theory.
7. Why are X-bar chart limits tighter than I-chart limits at the same 3-sigma multiple?
   One sentence, in terms of sigma_xbar = sigma/sqrt(n).
8. A CLT simulation on 1000 draws prints sd of means = 3.313 um exactly. The lab's
   honesty clause says this is a red flag. Why?
