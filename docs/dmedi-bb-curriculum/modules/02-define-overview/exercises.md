from `data/bore-inspection-dpmo.csv` and `data/order-lead-time.csv`.

3. Convert the 6-opportunity DPMO to a sigma level (1.5-shift convention). Show the
   z computation. (5 min)

   the 7-day target. Which of these three belongs in the Y statement, and why do
   the other two still matter? (10 min)
5. The sample mean lead time (17.8 d) differs from the pinned baseline (18.4 d).
   Give the two lawful explanations and state which one applies here. (5 min)
6. Draft the CTQ-3 Y statement using the pinned ceiling: baseline $41.80 unit cost,
   target = price $30.00 less 5% ROS. Name the OD you would measure it with.
   (10 min)
