# Worksheet 06 — FMEA-Lite Risk Register

Lighter than a full FMEA (Module 26 owns DFMEA): risk, likelihood (1-5),
impact (1-5), score L x I, mitigation, owner, gate where the mitigation must
be EVIDENCED (not just attempted). Filled model for the CPPC program; blank
grid below.
