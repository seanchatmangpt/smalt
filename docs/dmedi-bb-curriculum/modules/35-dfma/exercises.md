# Module 35 Exercises

1. **Sensitivity.** If the loaded labor rate rises to $96/h, recompute the total DFMA saving
   and its % of the $13.30 gap. Does the ranking of moves by dollar impact change?
2. **Fastener economics.** Each M3 screw costs $0.02 material but 7 s assembly. At what labor
   rate does the ASSEMBLY cost of one screw equal 10x its material cost? What does this say
   about "cheap parts" reasoning?
3. **Design rule extraction.** Write the DFA design rule that the bracket-merge move embodies,
   in one sentence a designer can apply without seeing this teardown.
4. ** DFMA vs process cost.** The residual gap after DFMA is $2.00/unit. Machining cycle time
   is 6.8 min on a $62/h machine. What cycle-time reduction (in seconds) closes $2.00/unit?
   Is that plausible against the takt analysis of Module 33?
5. **Anti-overclaim.** A teammate claims DFMA "closed the cost gap." Write the precise,
   receipted version of that claim with the numbers from the key.
