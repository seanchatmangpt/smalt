# Price-Point Derivation from Market Willingness-to-Pay — CTQ-3

The target-costing ceiling is derived from what the market will pay, minus the
profit the business requires — not from cost-plus on the current teardown.

## WTP evidence (Define phase, voice-of-customer)

| Segment | Units/yr | Conjoint median WTP | Price at which segment drops out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier-1 drone OEM (OEM-direct) | 6,500 | $31.80 | $33.00 |
| Aftermarket/repair distributor | 3,800 | $29.20 | $31.50 |
| Low-volume custom (job shop resell) | 1,700 | $27.40 | $29.00 |
| Blended (volume-weighted) | 12,000 | — | — |

Volume-weighted median WTP: (6,500 x 31.80 + 3,800 x 29.20 + 1,700 x 27.40) /
12,000 = $30.35.

## Price-point selection

Selected target price: **$30.00/unit** at 12,000 units/yr.

Rationale:

- Below the volume-weighted WTP median ($30.35) so the blended book closes.
- Above the low-volume segment's $27.40, so that segment is deliberately
  de-prioritized (job-shop resell carries the highest transaction cost/unit).
- Leaves distribution headroom under the Tier-1 dropout point ($33.00) for
  negotiated annual price-downs without re-opening the program.

## Required profit and allowable cost

- Required return on sales: 5% (CPPC finance policy for new-line launches).
- Required profit = 5% x $30.00 = **$1.50/unit**.
- Allowable cost = $30.00 - $1.50 = **$28.50/unit** — the CTQ-3 ceiling pinned in
  RUNNING-CASE.md. The court asserts this identity holds exactly.

## Sensitivity

| If price is | Allowable cost at 5% ROS | Gap vs $41.80 driven cost |
|---|---|---|
| $29.00 | $27.55 | $14.25 |
| $30.00 (selected) | $28.50 | $13.30 |
| $31.00 | $29.45 | $12.35 |

The $1 of price movement moves the gap by exactly $1.00 (profit scales with
price), which is why the price point is set from WTP evidence first and the cost
roadmap is sized against the resulting ceiling — not the reverse.
