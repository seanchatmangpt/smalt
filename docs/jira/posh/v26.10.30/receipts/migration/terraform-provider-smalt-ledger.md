(module `terraform-provider-smalt`, terraform-plugin-framework v1.19.0, address
`registry.terraform.io/hashicorp/azurerm`). Disk finding: the tree contains the

at this path; the Elixir-spike premise of POSH-007 does not match disk. The
protocol facts POSH-007 names (handshake `1|6|tcp|<addr>|grpc`, magic cookie,

carried by terraform-plugin-framework v1.19.0 inside the Go source, so they are
preserved by the `ported` rows below, not by a separate Elixir read.

Dispositions: `ported` (live Go provider source/workflow carried forward),
`discarded (+reason)` (regenerable local artifact), `keep-as-reference`
(usage/parity sample, not compiled into the provider).
